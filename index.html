Shuo Wu

Ph.D. Candidate, Electrical and Computer Engineering
University of Illinois Chicago

I study how to improve LLM reasoning under resource constraints, focusing on inference-time control, reasoning-efficient system design, and leveraging internal model signals for reliability.

Research Interests

My work explores how reasoning can be treated as a controllable and resource-aware process. I am particularly interested in improving the efficiency, faithfulness, and robustness of large language models through system-level design (e.g., cascades), retrieval mechanisms, and analysis of internal signals such as attention.

Selected Work

  • Interactive LLM Cascade — Improved weak-model accuracy (+33%) while reducing strong-model usage (~48%).
  • Attention-Guided RAG — Studying attention as a signal for faithfulness and retrieval quality.
  • Robust Reward Design (JAIR 2025) — Optimization-based framework for reliable decision-making in MDPs.
