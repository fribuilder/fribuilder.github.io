About me

I come from a mathematics background and am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering. I like problems that combine theory, systems, and real empirical behavior.

Right now, I’m especially drawn to LLM reasoning: what it is, how it can be shaped at inference time, and how internal signals or structured pipelines can improve outcomes without simply scaling brute-force compute.

What I work on

  • LLM reasoning and inference-time control
  • Reasoning efficiency through cascades, routing, and budget-aware design
  • RAG faithfulness and the role of internal signals such as attention
  • Optimization-driven ML, including MILP/MDP-based formulations
  • Reproducible evaluation, logging, ablations, and benchmarking pipelines

Selected work

  • Interactive LLM Cascade: improved weak-model accuracy while reducing strong-model calls and cost.
  • Attention-Guided RAG: studying whether attention can predict useful context for faithful generation.
  • Robust Reward Design: optimization-based methods for reliable decision-making in MDPs.
